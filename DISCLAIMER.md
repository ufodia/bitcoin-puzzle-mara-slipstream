# Disclaimer and authorized-use policy

PuzzleSlipstream is experimental security software provided **“AS IS”**, without warranty of any kind.

## Authorized use only

You may use the software only when you own the relevant private key and funds or have explicit legal authorization from the owner. The project does not include or support:

- private-key brute force, range scanning, key recovery, or puzzle solving;
- interception or replacement of another person's transaction;
- fee-sniping, mempool theft, or adversarial RBF tooling;
- public-mempool broadcast fallbacks;
- custody, escrow, investment, legal, or tax services.

A public “Bitcoin Puzzle” label does not by itself resolve every legal, contractual, jurisdictional, or ethical question. You are responsible for establishing your authority before using the software.

## Financial and operational risk

Bitcoin transactions are irreversible. Software defects, compromised build systems, incorrect UTXO data, fee errors, malicious relays, miner behavior, leaked keys, clipboard malware, firmware compromise, and operational mistakes can cause permanent loss. A private relay can reject, delay, leak, replace, or fail to mine a transaction.

## Independent review required

Do not rely on a release, checksum, CI badge, or maintainer statement as a security audit. Review the exact source revision, reproduce the build, test on Regtest and Testnet4, and obtain an independent audit before valuable mainnet use.
